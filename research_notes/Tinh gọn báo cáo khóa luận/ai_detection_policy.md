# AI-text detection (Turnitin), reliability, Vietnamese university AI policy, and honest-authorship practice — brief for an IUH thesis (researched 27/11/2026)

Scope note: research done 27/11/2026 with WebSearch/WebFetch. Turnitin's own guide pages (guides.turnitin.com, turnitin.com product pages) returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher, so Turnitin facts below come from Turnitin press releases and blog posts seen through search, plus university help pages and news coverage. Items marked "(search snippet)" were seen only in search-result summaries, not in a fetched page. **No IUH-specific regulation on AI use or similarity thresholds was found online.** That is the largest gap.

## 1. How Turnitin's AI writing detection works, which languages it covers, and how the score is shown

### Takeaway
Turnitin scores long-form prose sentence by sentence and reports the share of prose sentences it predicts are AI-generated. Since July 2024 it shows only an asterisk for scores from 1% to 19%. It needs at least 300 words of prose. Detection exists only for a fixed list of languages: English, Spanish and Japanese (April 2025), then Arabic, and Dutch/German/Turkish (October 2026). **Vietnamese is not on any list found.** Text in an unsupported language gets no AI report. AI-paraphrase detection (July 2024) and "AI bypasser"/humanizer detection (August 2025) exist, but bypasser detection works on English only.

### Cited Findings
- Turnitin describes the AI percentage as "the overall percentage of prose sentences contained in a long-form writing format within the submitted document that Turnitin's AI writing detection model determines was generated by AI" — quoted on university help pages in search results, e.g. [Univ. of Melbourne guide](https://lms.unimelb.edu.au/staff/guides/canvas/assignments/turnitin-accessing-and-interpreting-the-ai-writing-report) and [Turnitin help center: "Why is the AI score showing as *%?"](https://helpcenter.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/46245332050317-Why-is-the-AI-Writing-Detection-report-score-showing-as) (search snippet).
- Asterisk rule: "to avoid potential incidence of false positives, no score or highlights are attributed for AI detection scores above 0% and below the 20% threshold." Reports generated before 8 July 2024 may still show a number below 20% — [Turnitin help center](https://helpcenter.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/46245332050317-Why-is-the-AI-Writing-Detection-report-score-showing-as) (search snippet).
- The minimum length was raised from 150 to 300 words of prose "in a long-form writing format", because accuracy rises with length — Turnitin CPO, cited in [Higher Ed Dive, 7 Jun 2023](https://highereddive.com/news/turnitin-false-positives-AI-detector/652356) and university guides (search snippet).
- The detector scores prose in long-form writing. Because it requires long-form prose sentences, lists, tables, code and similar content are not the target. This is implied by the "prose sentences… long-form writing" definition above. I could not fetch Turnitin's exact exclusion list (403).
- In 2023 Turnitin said it saw more false positives at the beginning and end of documents and was changing how it aggregates sentences there. It also said that "real-world use is yielding different results from our lab" — [Higher Ed Dive, 7 Jun 2023](https://highereddive.com/news/turnitin-false-positives-AI-detector/652356).
- Turnitin's stated error rates (2023): document-level false-positive rate below 1% for documents with more than 20% AI writing, validated on 800,000 pre-GPT samples. Sentence-level false-positive rate about 4%. False-positive sentences cluster next to real AI text: 54% are adjacent to it and 26% are two sentences away — [Turnitin blog, "Understanding the false positive rate for sentences…"](https://www.turnitin.co.uk/blog/understanding-the-false-positive-rate-for-sentences-of-our-ai-writing-detection-capability) (via search summary). No independent audit of these figures was found.
- Japanese detection launched 2 April 2025 — [Turnitin press release](https://turnitin.com/press/turnitin-introduces-ai-writing-detection-for-japanese-submissions). Spanish followed in the same period — [Turnitin press](https://turnitin.com/press/turnitin-ai-writing-detection-spanish-submission). Arabic is also supported — [Turnitin AU press](https://www.turnitin.com.au/press/turnitin-expands-ai-writing-detection-to-support-arabic-language-submissions). Dutch, German and Turkish were added in October 2026 — [PR Newswire, Oct 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/da/pressemeddelelser/turnitin-now-offers-ai-writing-detection-for-dutch-german-and-turkish-language-submissions-302899374.html); [TechEdge AI](https://techedgeai.com/turnitin-expands-ai-detection-to-dutch-german-turkish/).
- Unsupported language: the detector "will not process the submission" and no AI report is generated. Mixed-language documents are not supported — Turnitin FAQ and a university library guide, as summarized in search results ([Turnitin FAQ](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/28477544839821-Turnitin-s-AI-writing-detection-capabilities); [YZU library notice](https://www.yzu.edu.tw/library/index.php/en-us/news-events/2445-is-202601051445)) (search snippet).
- No source found lists Vietnamese among Turnitin's AI-detection languages. A Turnitin help page says AI detection "will only work for content submitted in English" (older wording), and Turnitin says it will announce non-English support as it arrives — [Turnitin help](https://help.turnitin.com/ithenticate/user/ai-writing-detection.htm) (search snippet).
- AI-paraphrase detection (text from an AI that was then reworded by an AI paraphraser) was added in July 2024. "AI bypasser detection" for humanizer-tool output was announced in August 2025. It is turned on automatically with AI detection, is limited to Originality add-on and iThenticate 2.0 customers, and is **English only**. Turnitin's CPO repeated the claim of a false-positive rate below 1%. No outside testing of the bypasser feature had been published at that point — [Plagiarism Today, 27 Aug 2025](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/08/27/turnitin-launches-anti-ai-humanizer-feature/); [THE Journal, 2 Sep 2025](https://thejournal.com/Articles/2025/09/02/New-Turnitin-Bypasser-Detection-Feature-Helps-Identify-Use-of-AI-Humanizer-Tools.aspx).
- The AI score is separate from the similarity score, and AI highlights do not appear in the Similarity Report. Turnitin help text says the indicator "should be used for guidance only, not as a final judgement." It also warns that when students use AI tools to assist authentic writing (paraphrasing, grammar revision), Turnitin "may predict it as AI writing and provide false positives" — Turnitin help pages in search results, e.g. [Turnitin help: similarity report](https://help.turnitin.com/feedback-studio/d2l/instructor/the-similarity-report/refining-the-similarity-report.htm) (search snippet; the exact source page was not identified).
- Early usage (to 14 May 2023): 38.5M submissions; 9.6% had more than 20% AI writing and 3.5% had more than 80% — [Higher Ed Dive](https://highereddive.com/news/turnitin-false-positives-AI-detector/652356).

### Inferences
- **For a thesis written mainly in Vietnamese, Turnitin probably produces no AI score for the Vietnamese prose,** because Vietnamese is unsupported and mixed-language documents are not supported. An English abstract or English-only sections could be scored only if they reach about 300 words and the document as a whole is treated as English. Treat this as likely, not confirmed: the students should ask the IUH library or faculty which tool and settings are used.
- If IUH runs an "AI check" on Vietnamese text, it may use a different vendor, or Turnitin on a translated or English part. The reliability of any Vietnamese-language detector is undocumented (see §2).
- AI-assisted grammar and paraphrase edits are a known false-positive trigger even in English. Light AI "polishing" of English passages can raise the score even when the ideas are the students' own.

### Gaps
- Turnitin's current official language list and exclusion list (lists, tables, code, bibliography) could not be fetched (403). The maximum word count and file-size limits were not confirmed.
- Whether IUH's Turnitin licence includes the AI-writing or Originality add-on is unknown.

## 2. Independent evidence on detector reliability and institutional responses

### Takeaway
Independent studies find AI-text detectors neither accurate nor robust. In one study they were biased against non-native English writers, and paraphrasing defeats them. Several US universities turned Turnitin's AI detector off, citing false positives. One 2026 study, in Czech, did not reproduce the non-native bias for current detectors, so the bias finding should be cited with its date and setting.

### Cited Findings
- Liang, Yuksekgonul, Mao, Wu, Zou (2023), "GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers" (*Patterns*; arXiv Apr/Jul 2023). Seven detectors flagged 91 human-written TOEFL essays as AI with an **average false-positive rate of 61.22%**, while doing near-perfectly on US 8th-grade essays. 18/91 (19.78%) were flagged by all seven detectors and 89/91 (97.8%) by at least one. The bias was linked to low perplexity (limited vocabulary and syntax). Making the text more linguistically diverse removed the bias — [arXiv 2304.02819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02819); [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10382961/); [Stanford HAI](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/ai-detectors-biased-against-non-native-english-writers).
- Counter-evidence (2026): Al Ali, Helcl, Libovický, "Different Time, Different Language" (EACL 2026 SRW, arXiv 5 Feb 2026). For **Czech**, non-native texts did not have lower perplexity, three detector families showed "no systematic bias against non-native speakers", and current detectors do not rely on perplexity alone — [arXiv 2602.05769](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05769). This covers Czech only and says nothing about Vietnamese or Turnitin specifically.
- Weber-Wulff et al. (2023), "Testing of detection tools for AI-generated text," *Int. J. for Educational Integrity* 19:26. The study tested 12 public tools plus Turnitin and PlagiarismCheck. Conclusion: the tools "are neither accurate nor reliable", lean toward classifying output as human-written, and obfuscation (paraphrasing, machine translation) "significantly worsen[s]" performance — [arXiv 2306.15666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15666); [published PDF](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/207396/1/s40979-023-00146-z.pdf).
- Sadasivan et al., "Can AI-Generated Text be Reliably Detected?" (TMLR; arXiv v1 Mar 2023, v4 Jan 2025). Recursive paraphrasing substantially lowers detection rates for watermark, neural, zero-shot and retrieval detectors with little loss of quality. Watermarks can be spoofed so that human text gets flagged as AI. The best possible detector's AUROC is bounded by the total-variation distance between human and AI text distributions, so detection gets harder as models improve — [arXiv 2303.11156](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11156).
- Vanderbilt disabled Turnitin's AI detector on 16 Aug 2023 "for the foreseeable future". Its reasons: a 1% false-positive rate applied to about 75,000 papers a year means about 750 papers wrongly flagged; bias against non-native speakers; privacy and lack of transparency; and the conclusion that AI detection "is not a practical solution" — [Vanderbilt Brightspace blog](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/brightspace/2023/08/16/guidance-on-ai-detection-and-why-were-disabling-turnitins-ai-detector/); [Yahoo/Canadian coverage](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/universities-ditching-ai-detection-software-110514937.html).
- Michigan State and UT Austin also turned the detector off (2023) — [Bloomberg, 21 Sep 2023](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2023-09-21/universities-rethink-using-ai-writing-detectors-to-vet-students-work). Oregon State and UAB have also documented disabling it — [Oregon State UIT](https://uit-at.oregonstate.edu/turnitin-ai-detection-disabled); [UAB](https://sites.uab.edu/navigatinggenaipedagogy/ending-the-gen-ai-detection-war-turning-off-turnitin) (search results; dates not confirmed).
- In Vietnam: a Google-certified trainer quoted in Tuổi Trẻ (21/01/2026) said "nếu 100% chỉ dựa vào phần mềm phát hiện AI là không hiệu quả" and advised lecturers "không nên vội cáo buộc sinh viên", asking students to explain passages instead — [Tuổi Trẻ, 21/01/2026](https://tuoitre.vn/ai-vao-giang-duong-giang-vien-giu-liem-chinh-hoc-thuat-ra-sao-20260121162839203.htm).

### Inferences
- The strongest honest position for the students is that a detector score is probabilistic evidence. Their own vendor, independent researchers, several universities and Vietnamese regulations (UEH, §4) all say it cannot be used alone to establish misconduct.
- Formulaic, low-variety prose has been linked to false flags, at least in older detectors and in English. Writing with specific, varied, project-grounded sentences is good writing anyway and plausibly reduces false flags. There is no guarantee.

### Gaps
- No independent evaluation of Turnitin's 2025 bypasser detection, or of any detector on Vietnamese text, was found.
- Per-tool accuracy percentages from Weber-Wulff et al. were not extracted.

## 3. Similarity (đạo văn) score compared with AI detection: what raises it and what settings exclude

### Takeaway
The similarity index measures text matched against databases. It is a separate number from the AI score. In theses it is typically raised by quoted definitions, standard method descriptions, legal or terminology phrases, references lists and template text. Instructors can exclude bibliography, quoted text and small matches, and Vietnamese regulations (UEH) explicitly exclude citations, references, standard methods and terminology when judging plagiarism.

### Cited Findings
- Turnitin filters can exclude quoted text and bibliographic or reference material, and can exclude small matches by word count (for example under 20 words) or percentage (for example under 10%). One page gives a default minimum match length of 8 words — [Turnitin help: excluding quotes and bibliography](https://help.turnitin.com/feedback-studio/turnitin-website/instructor/similarity-report-exclusions/excluding-quotes-and-bibliography.htm); [Turnitin help: editing Similarity Report settings](https://help.turnitin.com/integrity/instructor/web/managing-files-and-folders/editing-similarity-report-settings.htm); [VCU exclusions guide](https://learningsystems.vcu.edu/instructional-tools/turnitin/faculty/similarity-reports-explained/report-exclusions) (search snippets).
- Edge cases: bibliography exclusion exists only in the classic report view, and newer views lean on the small-match filter. Single quotation marks are not treated as quotes. Indented block quotes are excluded for .doc/.docx — [Turnitin iThenticate definitions](https://help.turnitin.com/ithenticate/user/the-similarity-report/bibliography-and-quote-exclusion-definitions.htm) (search snippet).
- UEH (QĐ 4002/QĐ-ĐHKT-NCPTGKTC, 18/12/2025) treats a similarity rate "từ 20% trở lên" as a sign of possible violation, **excluding** citations, references, standard methods, terminology, legal citations and proper names. The Turnitin result is "chỉ mang tính tham khảo, hỗ trợ" — [UEH Đào tạo](https://daotao.ueh.edu.vn/quy-dinh-ve-viec-kiem-soat-xu-ly-hanh-vi-dao-van-su-dung-tri-tue-nhan-tao-ai-trong-cac-san-pham-hoc-thuat-tai-dai-hoc-kinh-te-thanh-pho-ho-chi-minh/).
- Thresholds vary across Vietnamese universities (20–30% commonly cited). One commercial blog recommends under 15% for coursework and theses. These are secondary or commercial sources, not regulations — [kiemtratailieu.vn](https://kiemtratailieu.vn/blog/tong-hop-quy-dinh-ve-kiem-tra-trung-lap-dao-van/); [luanvanonline.com](https://luanvanonline.com/nguong-dao-van-ai-bao-nhieu-la-an-toan/) (low reliability).
- A Vietnamese instructor's FAQ stresses that the software does not "convict" anyone of plagiarism; it only finds strings matching published work — [Thầy Phong Đặng](https://thayphongdang.edubit.vn/blog/check-dao-van-va-nhung-hieu-lam) (search snippet).

### Inferences
- For this thesis, likely sources of similarity are verbatim definitions (SED, PSDS, RAG, BGE-M3), dataset descriptions copied from the DataSEC/DataSED papers, license text, the faculty template's fixed text (cover, cam đoan, nhận xét pages), standard metric formulas, and the references list. Paraphrase definitions with a citation, or quote them with quotation marks and a citation. Ask whether the faculty's check excludes bibliography, quotes and small matches.
- Machine-translating an English source into Vietnamese does not make it original. Weber-Wulff et al. treat translation as an obfuscation technique.

### Gaps
- The IUH Faculty of Information Technology's similarity threshold (the students believe it is under 25%) and its exclusion settings were not found online.

## 4. Vietnamese context: MOET and university rules on AI in theses (2025–2026), disclosure requirements and wording

### Takeaway
National level: Thông tư 49/2026/TT-BGDĐT (in force 15/8/2026) names AI cheating, plagiarism, data fabrication and **failing to disclose AI use when required** as integrity violations. It makes each university issue its own AI code of conduct. Universities that have published rules (UEH Dec 2025, ĐH Mở TP.HCM Apr 2025, ULIS–VNU 2025) all allow some AI use but require disclosure. They forbid AI generating most of the work and fabricated citations, and UEH states that AI-detection results are not the sole basis for a violation finding. ULIS lists using AI to write a thesis or part of one as a form of plagiarism. **No IUH rule was found.**

### Cited Findings
- **Thông tư 49/2026/TT-BGDĐT** (MOET) on technology in higher and vocational education, effective 15/8/2026, replaces TT 15/2018 and TT 30/2023. AI is a "công cụ hỗ trợ, không thay thế vai trò và trách nhiệm học thuật của giảng viên". Listed integrity violations: using AI to cheat, copying or plagiarism, forging or distorting data, and "không công bố việc sử dụng công nghệ, trí tuệ nhân tạo khi có yêu cầu". Universities must issue internal academic-integrity rules and an AI code of conduct for training and research, and must keep assessment records traceable — [VietnamPlus](https://www.vietnamplus.vn/bo-gd-dt-ban-hanh-quy-dinh-ve-su-dung-ai-trong-dao-tao-nghien-cuu-khoa-hoc-post1121996.vnp); [LSVN](https://lsvn.vn/bo-giao-duc-va-dao-tao-lan-dau-quy-dinh-ve-su-dung-ai-trong-dao-tao-nghien-cuu-khoa-hoc-a175434.html).
- National AI law: Luật Trí tuệ nhân tạo số 134/2025/QH15 (10/12/2025) sets the general legal framework for AI — cited in search summaries ([quanlynhanuoc.vn](https://www.quanlynhanuoc.vn/?p=68913)) (search snippet; not verified in the law text).
- A draft higher-education training regulation (quy chế đào tạo) proposes that AI cheating can lead to discipline — [giaoduc.net.vn](https://giaoduc.net.vn/du-thao-quy-che-dao-tao-dai-hoc-gian-lan-bang-ai-co-the-bi-ky-luat-post260422.gd) (headline only; status not verified).
- **UEH, QĐ 4002/QĐ-ĐHKT-NCPTGKTC (18/12/2025)** — [UEH](https://daotao.ueh.edu.vn/quy-dinh-ve-viec-kiem-soat-xu-ly-hanh-vi-dao-van-su-dung-tri-tue-nhan-tao-ai-trong-cac-san-pham-hoc-thuat-tai-dai-hoc-kinh-te-thanh-pho-ho-chi-minh/):
  - Assistive AI (grammar, spelling, translation, sentence structure) that does not create content needs no disclosure. Generative AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) used for summarizing, translation, figures or code **must be disclosed**: tool, purpose, and what it supported. Sample wording for the Acknowledgement or Methods section: "Một phần nội dung của sản phẩm này được hỗ trợ bởi ChatGPT (phiên bản …)".
  - Prohibited: undisclosed or incomplete disclosure; using AI output without checking it; AI generating all or most of the product without substantive original contribution; fabricated data; fake citations; AI where prohibited; feeding sensitive data to AI.
  - Art. 5.1: UEH does not use AI-detection results as the "căn cứ duy nhất". AI cases are decided by the lecturer, supervisor or council **after a direct discussion with the author**, or by an alternative assessment.
  - Turnitin is mandatory for khóa luận/luận văn/luận án. Similarity of 20% or more (after exclusions) signals a possible violation. Before defense the student revises; at defense a council can mark the work "không đạt" pending revision.
  - Lecturers must document their grounds, test results and discussion records, and complaints go to the QA and Internal Control Office.
- **ĐH Mở TP.HCM, QĐ 623/QĐ-ĐHM (4/4/2025):** generative AI may not do all of an assignment, exam, thesis or dissertation, and AI-generated content may not exceed **20%** of the work. The university's student-research AI guide requires declaring tool, version and how it was used, and lets supervisors refuse submission on verified evidence of AI-rule violations — [VNReview summary](https://vnreview.vn/threads/70-sinh-vien-thua-nhan-dung-ai-de-lam-bai-tap-do-an-con-so-bao-dong-hay-tin-hieu-cua-su-thay-doi-tat-yeu.59707/latest); [HCMCOU guide PDF](https://htqlkh.ou.edu.vn/api/upload/hcmcou-ffMI35CSF-Huong%20dan%20AI%20cho%20SVNCKH.pdf) (search snippets; the PDF fetch failed on TLS).
- **ULIS–VNU, thesis plagiarism regulation (QĐ 3515/QĐ-ĐHNN, 10/7/2025; file "3559"):** plagiarism is "một lỗi nghiêm trọng về tiêu chuẩn đạo đức khoa học", and the listed forms include using AI applications to write the thesis or part of it — [ULIS PDF](https://student.ulis.vnu.edu.vn/files/uploads/2026/09/3559_Quy-dinh-kiem-soat-va-xu-ly-dao-van-doi-voi-sinh-vien-lam-KLTN.pdf) (search snippet).
- Nha Trang University (QĐ 2036, 30/12/2025) controls plagiarism in academic products including coursework and theses — [NTU PDF](https://pdtsaudaihoc.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/22/THU%20NGA/207-QD%20so%202036%20Kiem%20soat%20dao%20van%20cac%20sp%20hoc%20thuat%2030_12_25.pdf) (search snippet).
- A NEU faculty head (Tuổi Trẻ 21/01/2026) warns that content copied from AI without understanding or citation "có thể bị xem là đạo văn và dẫn đến điểm 0" — [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/ai-vao-giang-duong-giang-vien-giu-liem-chinh-hoc-thuat-ra-sao-20260121162839203.htm).
- No official ĐHQG-HCM generative-AI guideline text was found. A VNU-HN course document notes that ĐHQG-HCM and others have internal guidance — [VNU LMS PDF](https://cdnlms.vnu.edu.vn/dhqg.file.api/uploads/lms/document/25000265/20251213/tmp8cr3ax_bai6_cns.pdf) (search snippet). UNESCO's Vietnamese-language guidance on GenAI in education and research is available — [UNESDOC](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000393269).
- **International disclosure templates:**
  - Elsevier (policy updated June 2026): a section titled "Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process" with the template "During the preparation of this work, the author(s) used [NAME OF TOOL / SERVICE] in order to [REASON]. After using this tool/service, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the published article." Basic grammar, spelling and punctuation checks and reference managers need no declaration. AI may not be listed or cited as an author. Authors must verify output, including references, which can be fabricated — [Elsevier](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals).
  - APA: describe AI use in the Method section or a comparable section, possibly with the prompt and relevant output. Cite the tool as software (Manual §10.10), and follow the instructor's or publisher's rules — [APA Style blog](https://apastyle.apa.org/blog/how-to-cite-chatgpt); [APA: cite GenAI where allowed](https://apastyle.apa.org/blog/cite-generative-ai-allowed).
  - IEEE (Editorial Style Manual, March 2025): AI-generated content (text, figures, images, code) must be disclosed in the acknowledgments. Editing and grammar tools are generally outside the policy. There is no official IEEE citation format for AI output — [McMaster IEEE guide](https://libguides.mcmaster.ca/cite-gen-ai/ieee) (search snippet).

### Inferences
- An IUH declaration modelled on UEH plus Elsevier would read roughly like this (Vietnamese): "Trong quá trình thực hiện khóa luận, nhóm sử dụng [tên công cụ, phiên bản] để [mục đích cụ thể: rà soát ngữ pháp, gợi ý cấu trúc đoạn, hỗ trợ viết/sửa mã, dịch thuật ngữ…] ở [phần/chương]. Nhóm đã kiểm tra, chỉnh sửa toàn bộ nội dung, tự kiểm chứng mọi số liệu và trích dẫn, và chịu hoàn toàn trách nhiệm về nội dung khóa luận." The useful elements are: tool and version, purpose, which parts, what the students did themselves, verification of numbers and references, and acceptance of responsibility. Use any IUH or faculty form if one exists.
- If IUH follows TT 49/2026 (in force since 15/8/2026), IUH should have or be drafting an internal AI code. The students should ask the faculty or Phòng Đào tạo for it and follow it over any template above.

### Gaps
- IUH's own AI-use rule, its thesis similarity threshold (under 25% is the students' belief), whether an AI check runs and with which tool, and any required declaration form: none found online.
- The full text of TT 49/2026 was not read (news summary only).

## 5. Legitimate practices that make the writing the authors' own and lower the risk of a false flag; what to do if flagged

### Takeaway
The defensible approach combines three things: transparent disclosure; content only the authors could have written (their own decisions, numbers, failures and reasoning, in their own voice); and a verifiable authorship trail (git history, drafts, run logs). Do not use "humanizer" or bypass tools. Turnitin detects some of them, and policies such as UEH's treat concealment or incomplete disclosure as a violation in itself. If flagged, ask for the discussion that UEH-style rules require, and bring evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Detector bias was linked to low-variety, low-perplexity prose. Liang et al. found that adding linguistic diversity removed the bias — [arXiv 2304.02819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02819). (This supports writing with specifics and varied structure for quality reasons. It is not a claim that this defeats detection.)
- Turnitin says false-positive sentences cluster at transitions next to real AI text and at the start and end of documents — [Turnitin blog](https://www.turnitin.co.uk/blog/understanding-the-false-positive-rate-for-sentences-of-our-ai-writing-detection-capability); [Higher Ed Dive](https://highereddive.com/news/turnitin-false-positives-AI-detector/652356).
- Turnitin's 2025 bypasser detection targets text altered by humanizer tools, alongside the 2024 AI-paraphrase detection — [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/08/27/turnitin-launches-anti-ai-humanizer-feature/); [THE Journal](https://thejournal.com/Articles/2025/09/02/New-Turnitin-Bypasser-Detection-Feature-Helps-Identify-Use-of-AI-Humanizer-Tools.aspx).
- UEH forbids undisclosed or incomplete AI disclosure and fabricated citations. ### Inferences (practical checklist; derived from the sources above, not from any single source)
- **Rewrite in your own voice**, especially in the introduction, conclusion and transitions (the positions Turnitin associates with false positives). Prefer first-person-plural group decisions ("nhóm chọn… vì…") and concrete reasons over generic sentences.
- **Anchor claims in project-specific facts** that no AI could invent: actual run IDs and dates, measured numbers with CIs, negative results, bugs found and fixed, decisions logged in ADRs, and dev/test discipline. This project already has an unusually strong trail: ADRs, PAPER_NOTES, measurement files generated by scripts, the test ledger, artifact freezes, and commit history.
- **Keep the authorship evidence**: git log with dated commits for report sources (`report/final_src/`), drafts, the build scripts that generate tables and figures, and meeting or supervisor notes. In a dispute, show the diff history of a flagged paragraph.
- **Verify every citation and number yourselves.** UEH and Elsevier both name fabricated references as misconduct. Check any reference touched by AI against Crossref or the PDF.
- **Quote correctly**: quotation marks plus citation for verbatim definitions, paraphrase plus citation otherwise. Use real double quotes, since Turnitin does not treat single quotes as quotations.
- **Disclose fully and specifically** (§4 template). Incomplete disclosure is itself a violation under UEH's rule and TT 49's "khi có yêu cầu" clause.
- **Do not use humanizer, paraphraser-to-evade or bypass tools, and do not translate text back and forth to evade checks.** It is deceptive, Turnitin specifically targets it (English), it conflicts with disclosure duties, and it can degrade accuracy.
- **If flagged**: ask for the full report (similarity sources, or the AI-highlighted sentences, noting that scores below 20% show only "*%"). Ask which tool and language mode was used, since Turnitin's AI model does not support Vietnamese. Present the AI declaration, git and draft history, generated-artifact provenance and supervisor confirmation. Offer an oral explanation of any flagged passage. Cite the vendor's own caveat (the score is guidance, not a final judgement) and the independent studies in §2. Follow the formal complaint route (Phòng Đào tạo or the QA unit). The UEH structure illustrates what a fair process looks like.
